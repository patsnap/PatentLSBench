# LS Research

[PatentLSBench](https://github.com/patsnap/PatentLSBench) · **English** | [简体中文](README.zh.md) · [Evaluation Protocol](../docs/evaluation-protocol.md)

LS Research evaluates evidence-based answers to life-sciences research questions.

## Input

A research question with its requested objects, scope, and information cutoff.

## Output

A complete answer or research report with source citations supporting its factual conclusions.

## Evaluation

- **Key-point coverage:** whether the response addresses the question and includes the necessary findings.
- **Factual quality:** whether claims, object identities, comparisons, and conclusions are accurate within the requested scope.
- **Citation support:** whether sources are relevant, traceable, and support the associated claims.

Task-specific reference evidence and rubrics support assessment of the complete answer and its citations.
